---
title: I shall kneel before you
date: 2019-10-28
excerpt:
---

I shall kneel before you,
a flower in my hand.

The waves of life
will rise beneath the breeze of tenderness.

I shall plunge into
love's deepest whirlpool.

The harp of silence
will make me dance to the rhythm of passion.

I shall shatter every cliché,
every paradox.

I shall show you rays of light
ringing like bells from a distant steeple.

I shall share with you
all my dreams, all my wishes.

And barefoot,
I shall lead you along a thorn-covered path.
