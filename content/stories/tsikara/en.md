date: 2021-03-30

The matador stands before me, holding a long, slender sword. I'm already losing blood, and I have almost no strength left, but my instincts keep telling me to charge again and again. And now, in these final moments, even more painful memories come flooding back.

I remember being born in Texas, out in the open fields. I gave thanks to something, though I don't know what, but I was happy just to be alive. I felt wonderful, and that feeling didn't last long.

One day, something flew over our heads, and soon after, I fell asleep. When I woke up, it was already dark. I was alone, and in a frenzy, I began throwing myself from side to side. Before long, I fell asleep again.

I was still quite dazed when I woke up for the second time. Now I was behind bars. There was enough room to stretch my legs, but nowhere near as much as there had been in the fields. After running around for a while and fully waking up, I looked beyond the bars. That's when I saw them: people dressed alike, wearing boots, jeans, shirts, and large hats. Some were smiling, others laughing, others talking, but every gaze was fixed on me.

After days of good food and running around inside my enclosure, one morning I began hearing a commotion. More and more people came to see me, and my tension slowly grew. Suddenly, the bars began closing in, and a door opened in front of me, as if I were supposed to go through it. So I did.

Before long, I found myself in a brightly lit place, even louder than before. My anxiety grew. There was nothing I could do but keep moving forward, and soon I found myself in a space barely large enough to hold me.

That was when I felt something touch my back. Two legs slid down along my sides, and I went wild, but I couldn't turn around. Staying there was unbearable. I tried everything, struggling to move within the narrow enclosure and throw off whoever was on top of me, but I still couldn't move. My ears were ringing.

Then, suddenly, one side of the enclosure opened, and I burst out with all the force I could muster. I forgot everything else. All I could think about was throwing off the person riding on my back. I jumped higher and higher, kicking harder and harder, but nothing worked.

Again and again, until at last I could no longer feel anyone on my back. Immediately, I heard the unbearable, irritating, frenzied cheers and shouts of the crowd. Once again, every eye was fixed on me.

This happened several times over the course of that month.

One day, as I stood there, tense and alert, eating grass, a stranger appeared. He was smiling at me, and unlike anyone else, he looked completely different. His clothes and facial features marked him as a true foreigner, and his smile was innocent. It was almost as if he had come to save me.

And indeed, soon afterward, I fell asleep and woke up somewhere entirely different.

I don't know where I was, but I was behind bars again, and even the grass tasted different. The stranger visited me several times. I would ask him with my eyes why I was there and why he hadn't set me free. There was no answer. He would only smile.

And now, after fighting several toreros, I have colorful things stuck into my back. Blood still pours so heavily from my wounds that it runs into my eyes, and I can barely see. But I can make out the smiling stranger standing before me, holding a long, slender sword.

Once again, every eye is fixed on me, and I know I must charge one more time.

I take a few steps forward and feel the touch of something cold and sharp.

It's over.
