title: The Red Door
date: 2026-10-12

---

She painted the door red the day he left.

Not a gentle red. Not the colour of roses, or of the curtains her mother had sewn. It was the red of a warning sign — the kind of red that refuses to be ignored.

The neighbours watched from their balconies. Nobody asked. On our street you do not ask; you wait until the story comes to you by itself, the way rain finds its way through an old roof.

For seven days the door stayed shut. Then, on Sunday, it opened, and she came out carrying a chair. She set it on the pavement and sat down in the sun as if she had been waiting her whole life for that exact square of light.

***

He came back in the spring. He stood at the gate for a long time, looking at the door as if it belonged to a stranger's house.

"You changed it," he said.

"No," she said, without getting up from her chair. "I changed *myself*. The door just agreed."
