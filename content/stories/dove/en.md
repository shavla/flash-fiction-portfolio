---
title: Where did the white doves go??
date: 2022-06-02
excerpt:
---

Yes, that is the title, and I have no idea where the pigeons went.

All I remember from childhood is how wonderful it was to come running home, drenched in sweat, and look out from the balcony at them. They seemed like dancing angels, and everything around them rejoiced — everything and everyone, including me.

Watching them, I could no longer feel my legs, nor my arms. It was as if they had never belonged to me in the first place, and all I could hear was the beating of my heart.

But what good is a heart now? We don't listen to it anyway. They're building another apartment block next door.

Their cooing, along with the sounds of their flapping wings, would wrap itself around your soul. Together, you would drift into infinity, promised immortality. Every second felt like a century, and every century like a second.

But what good is that now? More and more cables are wrapping themselves around the walls of the apartment block, dragging the soul down into an endless sea, drowning it, killing it.

Looking at their whiteness, you would immediately feel that you were human, different from all the countless other species of monkeys.

And then, one day, you look up and the white pigeons are gone. You have no idea whether they disappeared or what became of them. Instead, you notice your neighbour has installed something new on the roof of his garage: an antenna, whiter than the pigeons ever were.
