---
title: A Holy Day or a Day of Miracles?
date: 2020-09-10
excerpt:
---
A day of miracles, I say. There are plenty of holidays, but days of miracles are few and far between. Quality matters, not quantity. I remember two miraculous days, and I will never forget them. One turned my life right side up, and the other turned it upside down.

Every thoughtful person must have asked themselves at least once why they came into this world, and for what, or for whom, they should one day leave it. Naturally, I have asked myself these questions many times, too. And I remember that miraculous day when I heard my daughter cry for the first time. It was as if this was what I had been searching for all my life. This was why I had come into this world, and for whom I would one day leave it. A candle had been lit in a darkened room, and suddenly I could see everything. Her mother was a miracle, of course, but this tiny little creature was an even greater one. Life had begun turning right side up...

Every uphill climb is followed by a descent. That upward turn didn't last long, and then something happened that I could never have imagined. My life began turning upside down.

I stand in a cemetery on a rainy day, covered in mud, unable even to throw a handful of earth over them. Everything had turned into one enormous mass of mud, and it was doing nothing but staining me and dragging me under. The mud had even crept into my nostrils, and I could feel an unbearable cold and loneliness. I couldn't climb out of it. I couldn't move an inch...

There is a Japanese proverb that says two miraculous days come to every person's life, on two different occasions. I've already had my two miraculous days, and perhaps it would be better if I left this world soon.

All I hear now is the click of the hammer.
